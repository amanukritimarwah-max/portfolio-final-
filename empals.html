The core idea

A journey of comfort and growth, not just a lesson.

Learning breaks down not for lack of ability, but because the environment does not support executive function, emotional safety and adaptive pacing. EmPals fixes the environment, not the child.

It is about short tasks, instant feedback and meaningful rewards that build trust, confidence and a love for learning, because learning shouldn’t feel like a struggle.

It should feel like play.

Insights

We design for attention, effort and outcomes. But when it comes to feelings, safety and the first step, the system goes silent.

Environment as a factor

Children don’t refuse to learn. The place and pace around them make it hard to begin. Fix the environment, not the child.

Motivation as a factor

“First work, then play.” Short, achievable tasks unlock fun, so starting becomes easier.

Safety as a factor

No negative feedback. Mistakes are part of learning, and emotional safety comes before instruction.

Each factor is covered in the system of learning… except how a child actually feels about it.

Character design & form

  • Alex is a fox in round glasses: curious, kind and a little bookish. A guide, not a teacher.
  • Large expressive eyes and a wide smile keep the focus on emotion, so a child can read his mood at once.
  • Soft, rounded shapes and warm orange keep the screen friendly without feeling childish.

Scene & story

  • A forest path leads in, so learning feels like a small journey rather than a test.
  • Speech bubbles carry one short instruction at a time, in plain words.
  • One clear “Continue” button per screen keeps the next step obvious.
